# BatchBite: Kanban Board (Sprint 1)

This folder documents how the team tracked Sprint 1 on its Kanban board, with screenshots
from the beginning, middle and end of the sprint, and the meeting minutes in
[`meeting-minutes.md`](meeting-minutes.md).

**Board link:** https://github.com/users/noikilluk/projects/2
**Sprint 1 goal:** Completing UML, Wireframes and requirements

## How the board is set up

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Backlog | Work planned for later sprints (for example ingredient planning, QR collection) |
| Sprint 1 To Do | Cards selected for Sprint 1 and not started |
| In Progress | Someone is actively working on the card |
| In Review / Testing | Work is finished and waiting for a teammate to check it |
| Done | Reviewed, any feedback fixed, and merged or published |

_Edit the column names if your board uses different ones._

- **One card per piece of work**, written as a short title with a description and a link to the
  related requirement (for example US-02 or FR-06).
- **Labels:** `documentation`, `design`, `setup`.
- **Assignee:** every card has an owner.
- **Review:** a wireframe, diagram or document moves to In Review / Testing so that someone
  other than its author checks it. Feedback is left as a comment on the card.
